Weather builds every forecast from two free, open sources at once: Open-Meteo and MET Norway. Where both have a value it shows their average, and you can always see what each one said.

One forecast from two sources
• Temperature, feels like, rain, wind, humidity, UV and visibility, hour by hour and for 16 days
• A small dot where the two sources disagree; tap the temperature to see both values
• Compare sources: both forecasts side by side for the next 48 hours, and their highs and lows

Know what is coming
• Rain starting or stopping in the next two hours, from radar in the Nordics and from 15-minute forecasts elsewhere
• Weather warnings from MET Norway in Norway
• How today compares with the usual high for the date over the past 10 years
• Air quality (US AQI and PM2.5)

Your places
• Search for any city, or use your location without Google services
• Celsius or Fahrenheit, and km/h, m/s, mph or knots

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• The app only fetches while you use it
• Weather data by Open-Meteo.com and MET Norway (CC BY 4.0); place names from GeoNames; past temperatures from ERA5 and air quality from CAMS (Copernicus)
• Free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence: github.com/PVOT-OSS/PvotWeather
